# ORBITAL v0.1.1 — focused validation of the Web Alpha presets

**Additive release. No existing behaviour changes.** `constants`, `dynamics`, `propagate`, `diagnostics` and `_validation` are byte-identical to `v0.1.0`. The v0.1.0 tolerances T1–T12 are unchanged.

## What's new

- **`orbital.analytic`**: exact two-body solutions (AN-1 … AN-6). It covers orbital elements, classification, apsides, period, exact state at any time (Kepler's equation, elliptic and hyperbolic) and time to reach a radius.
- **An independent 50-digit reference**, `scripts/reference_mp.py` (mpmath, dev-only). It uses a different algorithm (universal variables), and its values are stored in `fixtures/v1/reference/`. REF-1 compares the two: worst disagreement 8.1 × 10⁻¹⁵ relative (gate 10⁻¹²).
- **Validation of all six Web Alpha presets** against plans and tolerances registered before any case ran: **6/6 pass**, including every gated prediction. See `docs/validation/v0.1.1-web-alpha-presets.md`.

## Kepler solver fix and analytic domain (Batch B, before release)

- **Defect.** `state_at` raised "Kepler's equation (elliptic) did not converge" for highly eccentric ellipses, for example speed factor 1.41 from 400 km (e = 0.9881).
  - Near periapsis the residual E − e sin E − M cancels, and its roundoff divided by the small slope 1 − e cos E is about 1.4e-15.
  - That exceeds the AN-5 test |ΔE| ≤ 1e-15, so Newton cycled at ±1 ulp until its 100 iterations ran out.
  - The numerical propagator never calls the solver and was not affected.
- **Fix, in both Python and TS.**
  - The residual is computed as (1 − e)E + e(E − sin E) − M and the slope as (1 − e) + e(1 − cos E). Neither cancels; this is the same treatment the hyperbolic solver already had.
  - The equation is odd, so the solver works on |M|.
  - Newton starts above the root, at min(π, |M| + e). Kepler's equation is increasing and convex there, so the iteration decreases monotonically to the root.
  - The AN-5 criterion, the iteration limit and the error are unchanged.
- **Domain.** e < 1 − 1e-9 is elliptic and e > 1 + 1e-9 is hyperbolic. Inside |e − 1| < 1e-9, `ValueError` is raised (AN-5). The web app then shows the computed run and states that there is no exact overlay.
- **Accuracy across the threshold.** Measured against an independent 50-digit universal-variable reference (`fixtures/v1/domain/conics.json`; tests `tests/test_conic_domain.py`, `web/packages/engine/test/domain.test.ts`):
  - REF-1 (1e-12) holds away from the threshold.
  - Near it, the error grows as about ε/|1 − e|. That growth is the conditioning of float64 elements, since energy v²/2 − μ/r cancels; it is not the solver.
  - The tests use 1e-12 + 4ε/|1 − e| + 8ε|v||t|/|r|.
  - The solver alone matches mpmath to 4ε relative at the same float64 M, for e up to 1 − 2e-9.
- **Unchanged.** The registered results and the REF-1 case references are untouched. The conformance fixtures changed only in `source_sha256`.
